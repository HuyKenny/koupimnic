# Graphics: koupimnic.cz

All illustrations, icons and the logo mark were generated in Canva (Canva AI image generation, site owner's account)
on 14. 11. 2026 for koupimnic.cz. They were exported at full resolution from the Canva design `DAHaRE_5Ko4`, which is
the shared scratch design also used for damenic and vian, and then processed on the Pi with Pillow: white background cut
out, trimmed, centred and resized. Canva export URLs are temporary and are never hot-linked. Licence: Canva content
licence of the account.

Palette: ink #1A1423, plum #4B1D6B, light plum #7A3FA0, lime #C8F169, coral #FF6B4A, paper #F7F5F2.
Typography in the logo and OG card: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold and Instrument Sans (Google Fonts, SIL OFL 1.1).
Both are rendered by Pillow, not by Canva.
